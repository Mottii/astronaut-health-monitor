# Astronaut Health Monitor (`astronaut-health-monitor`)

> **2026 NASA International Space Apps Challenge — Almaty Local Event**
> **Challenge:** [Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/health-monitoring-software/)

Autonomous astronaut health monitoring, interactive anatomical assessment, and space-medicine triage software designed for long-duration missions across orbital stations, deep-space transit vehicles, planetary rovers, and Lunar/Martian surface habitats.

---

## Overview

Long-duration missions expose crew members to NASA's **5 Hazards of Human Spaceflight**:
1. **Space Radiation**
2. **Isolation and Confinement**
3. **Distance from Earth**
4. **Gravity Fields (Altered Gravity)**
5. **Hostile Closed Environments**

Because communication latency and distance from Earth require crew autonomy, `astronaut-health-monitor` empowers astronauts to track physiological and behavioral health indicators (heart rate, blood pressure, blood glucose in `mg/dL`, body temperature, sleep hours, mood, radiation dosimetry, and diagnostic imaging), evaluate organ-specific risks on an interactive human body figure, and execute evidence-based NASA HRP countermeasures.
